\section{The Real Number System}

% ----- Concept map (structure note) -----
% A per-chapter map of the main results and how they depend on one another.
% Each entry links to its statement with \cref; keep it to the load-bearing
% concepts, not every result.
\begin{quote}\small\itshape
\textbf{Concept map.}
$\R$ is a \emph{complete ordered field}, built in three layers:
the field axioms (\cref{def:field}), the order axioms
(\cref{def:order-properties}), and completeness
(\cref{def:completeness-preview}, restated as the working axiom in
\S5). Completeness is the one axiom $\Q$ fails, and every later result
traces back to it: the Archimedean property (\cref{def:archimedean})
comes from completeness plus induction; it in turn drives the
consequences in \cref{cor:archimedean-consequences}, the existence of
$\sqrt2$ (\cref{prop:sqrt2-exists}), and the density of $\Q$
(\cref{thm:density-Q}). Suprema and infima
(\cref{def:supremum,def:infimum}) with their $\epsilon$-characterization
(\cref{lem:eps-characterization}) are the tools that make these
arguments run, and they return in \S3 as the engine of the monotone
convergence theorem.
\end{quote}
